Mission

Shaping the next generation of fashion faces

Perfect Modeling School combines international runway standards with Ethiopian culture and heritage. Every student gets personal mentoring, professional shoots and real stage experience before graduating.

Our trainers are working models, choreographers, photographers and stylists who bring real industry experience into every class.

FAQ

Good questions

Do I need experience to join?

No. Most programs start from zero. We assess your level at the interview and place you in the right group.

Is there an age limit?

We train students from 8 to 60. Kids and Teens has its own safe, parent-friendly program.

Will I get professional photos?

Yes. Every student builds a portfolio with studio and outdoor shoots led by our photographers.

Can I join part-time?

Evening and weekend classes are available. Private coaching has a fully flexible schedule.
